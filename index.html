Byculla Zoo Interactive Map

Byculla Zoo Navigator is an independent interactive map and visitor guide for Veermata Jijabai Bhosale Udyan (Byculla Zoo) in Mumbai, India. Open the map above to explore the grounds. This project is not the official zoo website.

How to use the interactive map

Pan and zoom to learn the layout, tap places for details, and use Locate when you want the map to show your browser GPS position. For step-by-step controls, read how to use the Byculla Zoo map.

Attraction discovery

Filter animals, birds, gardens, habitats, venues, and amenities, then open a place to learn more. See how attraction discovery works on the visitor map.

GPS and navigation

With location permission, the map can show where you are inside the zoo. Walking directions are offered only along administrator-verified visitor-accessible paths and only to verified attraction entrances. If those conditions are missing, no route is invented.

Visitor guide

For practical orientation tips and project limits, open the Byculla Zoo visitor guide. Map shapes and many names come from OpenStreetMap; basemap tiles come from OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap contributors.
