Byculla Zoo Visitor Guide

This independent guide helps visitors explore Veermata Jijabai Bhosale Udyan (Byculla Zoo) in Mumbai with an interactive map. It is not the official zoo website and does not publish ticket prices, opening hours, or official notices.

Open the interactive map

What this guide covers

Use the map to understand the zoo layout, browse places that appear in the visitor inventory, and orient yourself while you walk. The project focuses on map exploration and navigation support rather than official visitor services.

Before you rely on navigation

  • Walking routes appear only on paths marked visitor-accessible by an administrator.
  • Routes end only at attraction entrances that have been explicitly placed and verified.
  • Browser location permission is required if you want the map to show your position.
  • Location accuracy can vary indoors, under trees, or near tall buildings.

Data sources and limits

Map shapes and many place names come from OpenStreetMap. Basemap tiles are provided by OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap contributors. Availability labels on attractions reflect the project’s map data, not a live official feed from the zoo.

Byculla Zoo Navigator is an independent open-source visitor project. It is not affiliated with Byculla Zoo administration or the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation.
