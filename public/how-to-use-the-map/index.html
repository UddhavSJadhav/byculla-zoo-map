How to Use the Byculla Zoo Interactive Map

The homepage opens straight into the visitor map for Veermata Jijabai Bhosale Udyan (Byculla Zoo). Use these steps to explore the layout, find attractions, and request navigation when the required path and entrance data are ready.

Launch the map

Explore the layout

  • Drag to pan and pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • The visitor view stays focused on the zoo grounds.
  • Path styling helps you see walking routes that have been reviewed for visitor access.

Discover attractions

  • Tap a place mark or shape to open its details.
  • Use category filters to focus on animals, birds, gardens, venues, or amenities.
  • Availability text comes from the project’s attraction records, not a live official feed.

Use GPS

  • Tap the locate control and allow location access in your browser.
  • When a fix is available, the map can show your position inside the zoo.
  • If accuracy is poor, wait for a steadier reading before trusting the marker.

Request walking navigation

  • From an attraction’s details, choose Travel here when that action is offered.
  • A route is drawn only if your GPS can connect to the visitor-accessible path network.
  • The destination must have a verified entrance that sits on that network.
  • If those conditions are not met, the app explains why a route is unavailable instead of guessing.
