Byculla Zoo Attractions on the Map

The visitor map highlights places from the project’s attraction inventory for Veermata Jijabai Bhosale Udyan (Byculla Zoo). Tap a place on the map to read its details and, when a verified entrance is available, request a walking route.

Explore attractions on the map

Attraction categories

Filters on the visitor map group places into practical categories so you can focus on what you want to see. The current categories include:

  • Animals
  • Birds
  • Gardens
  • Habitats
  • Venues
  • Amenities such as places to eat and washrooms when they are mapped

How discovery works

  • Pan and zoom to scan the zoo layout.
  • Use category filters to show or hide groups of places.
  • Open an attraction to see its name, category, and any availability note stored in the map data.
  • Closed places can stay hidden unless you choose to show them.

What this page does not claim

This page does not list live animal sightings, temporary closures from the zoo, ticket information, or official facility schedules. Those details change and belong with the zoo’s own channels. The map only reflects attraction records maintained in this independent project.
